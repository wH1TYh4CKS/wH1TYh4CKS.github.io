Podmetrix

A local AI that watches your footage so you don't have to.

A creator records three hours on Monday and edits on Friday, having forgotten what happened. Before cutting anything they scrub the whole file linearly. Podmetrix indexes the recording into labelled, timestamped markers and imports them straight into Resolve, Premiere, Final Cut or YouTube.

It is a mapper, not a highlight-cutter: it never drops a stretch. Dull ones get labelled too, so a gap in the index always means "nothing there", never "the model missed it".

41 → 75%recall, after raising the frame budget

41.5smachine pass vs 10 min by hand

The interesting part is the measurement harness. There is no public dataset of "moments a creator would bookmark", so I hand-marked a 30-minute slice and built an LLM-judge pipeline to score against it — with audit sheets, because the judge was wrong often enough to need checking.

Honest limits: recall is measured on one slice of one video. A prompt-variant sweep across four videos came back statistically insignificant on every arm — the prompt was not where the headroom lived. That negative result is kept in the repo, because it is the reason the design is what it is.

MedicScribe

A medical scribe that speaks Manglish, and keeps nothing.

Off-the-shelf medical scribes assume a monolingual English consultation. A Malaysian primary-care visit mixes English, Mandarin, Bahasa Melayu and Tamil inside a single sentence. A doctor presses Record; audio streams to a GPU server on the clinic's own network; a structured SOAP note comes back in under 30 seconds.

4languages, code-switched mid-sentence
0bytes retained after a consultation
<30sfrom Stop to a finished note

The regulatory constraint was harder than the accuracy one. Under Malaysia's PDPA, a scribe that uploads consultation audio makes the clinic responsible for a data flow it cannot inspect. So privacy is enforced in code, not in a policy PDF: no database, audio unlinked at Stop, transcript in RAM only, a TTL reaper for crash orphans, and a live /transparency endpoint that reports the actual file count so the claim is checkable from outside.

Design decision worth defending: rather than detect the language and route to a model, one Whisper pass with task: translate turns any input language into an English note. No picker, no second hop. A Malaysian fine-tune was tested for this and rejected, and the measurements that killed it are committed next to the setting they justify.

Built and field-tested on real consultations; the clinic pitch was declined on legal and ethics grounds. It works, and it is not deployed. Not a medical device, not certified, not fit for clinical use.

How I work

  • Measure before optimising. Both projects ship a harness, not a demo reel. Podmetrix's recall jump came from discovering the model was not blind, it was under-fed — which only a measurement could tell me.
  • Keep the negative results. Rejected models, insignificant sweeps and confidently-wrong failure modes stay in the repos, next to the settings they explain.
  • Make claims checkable. Podmetrix has a test that fails the build if an undeclared outbound host appears in the source. MedicScribe exposes its own retention state over HTTP. A privacy promise you cannot verify is marketing.
  • Own the stack. Everything runs on a 4×3090 rig here — model serving, quantisation, multi-GPU topology, thermal limits. No inference bills, no data leaving the box.
